Case study 01 · Microsoft · AI Governance

Governing 5,000+ AI assets, without the chaos

OneRAI is Microsoft's internal platform for AI compliance review — the single destination product teams use to register, assess, and get approval for every AI system across the company. I led a ground-up redesign that turned a black-box review process into a guided, trackable flow, then prototyped an AI agent to push review time down even further.

Role

Lead Product Designer

Timeline

4 weeks

Team

4 PMs, 5 engineers

Tools

Figma, Figma Make

Overview

OneRAI is Microsoft's single destination for AI compliance — registering, reviewing, and approving every AI system the company ships. By the time I joined, it covered 5,000+ systems, but teams had little sense of what was required of them or how long approval would take.

The problem

The easy fix was copying the old flow onto the new platform. I pushed for more: teams didn't know what came after registering a model, assessments duplicated data they'd already given us, and once submitted, a review vanished into a black box with no visibility.

Goals & constraints

Goal

Give product teams a guided, intuitive path from registration to approval

Goal

Cut the time and manual effort required to complete an assessment

Constraint

Ship in 4 weeks with a team of 4 PMs and 5 engineers

Process

I mapped the end-to-end journey with product teams, review leads, and board members before drawing a single screen — aligning everyone around one source of truth. That surfaced three recurring pain points, which became the redesign:

  • Guided experience. A dynamic checklist and progress indicator, so teams always know what's next.
  • Faster assessments. API integration to import data instead of re-entering it, cutting completion time from 30 days to 4–5.
  • Status visibility. Self-serve progress tracking, replacing a black box with no way to check in.
  • Homepage & IA revamp. Made the whole platform easier to navigate.

Outcome

The redesign shipped as OneRAI's general release and is now the system of record for compliance review across every Microsoft engineering division. I also prototyped OneRAI Agent, a conversational layer that uses AI to help fill in assessments, surface open questions, and recommend next steps — aimed at cutting the end-to-end review time from 25–30 days down to 2–3.
