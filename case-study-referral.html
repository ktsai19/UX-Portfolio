Case study 02 · Chegg Uversity · Growth

Driving acquisition through trusted referrals

Educators didn't trust the Chegg brand, and sales couldn't scale one-to-one outreach to reach them. I designed a self-serve referral program for Chegg Uversity that turned existing instructors into the company's most credible acquisition channel.

Role

Lead Product Designer

Timeline

6 weeks

Team

Product, Eng, Finance, BizOps, Sales

Tools

Figma

Overview

Chegg reaches 8.1M+ monthly subscribers and 86% brand awareness with students — but educators were a different story. Instructors didn't trust Chegg's consumer products, and sales couldn't reach them one by one. We needed a self-serve way to earn trust and grow the instructor base.

The problem

Word of mouth from a trusted colleague was the one channel that could overcome that distrust. But people's past experience with referral programs was bad — unclear payouts, no visibility, no sense of when they'd get paid. Any solution had to build trust twice: in Chegg, and in the mechanic itself.

Goals & constraints

Goal

Give educators a self-serve way to refer colleagues, no sales involvement required

Goal

Make referral status and payout completely transparent

Constraint

Align legal, finance, BizOps, sales, and engineering on incentive structure and guardrails before any design work

Process

I ran cross-functional workshops with product, engineering, finance, BizOps, and sales to align on incentive, eligibility, and legal guardrails — keeping a shared user-flow artifact as requirements shifted. Research with educators shaped the design from there:

  • Built for trust. A persistent view of referral status and earnings, so users never had to contact support to check in.
  • Built for busy educators. A mobile-first layout, since 80% of traffic came from between classes.
  • Made it discoverable. Moved referral off the homepage onto its own dedicated page after early testing showed it got lost.
  • Adapted under pressure. A late engineering constraint forced a pivot from referral codes to shareable links — redesigned in days without slipping the launch.

Outcome

The referral program launched as part of Chegg Uversity and became one of the most effective self-serve acquisition channels for instructors — doubling onboarding completion and engagement, lowering the cost of acquiring a new educator, and driving 10% of new signups through referral within the first three months.
