How I work

Design philosophy

Four principles that show up in every AI, consumer, and enterprise product I've shipped.

01

System level thinking

I design for the pattern that has to hold at scale — the review flow spanning 5,000+ AI products at Microsoft, not just the screen in front of me.

02

Design for emotions

Whether it's a student asking an AI for help or a reviewer clearing a compliance flag, I design for how the moment feels, not just whether it works.

03

Transparency creates trust

People trust systems they can see into — clear reasoning over a black box, whether that's an AI agent's decision or a search result's ranking.

04

Don't make me think

A good interface gets out of the way. I look for the small moments where someone has to stop and think, and try to design those away.
