Case study 03 · Chegg · 0→1 AI Product

Designing for a new student learning paradigm in the world of AI

When ChatGPT put Chegg's core business at risk almost overnight, I led end-to-end design on CheggMate — a 0→1 conversational AI product built in six weeks and shipped to millions of students, backed by Chegg's network of 150,000+ subject-matter experts.

Role

Lead Designer

Timeline

Vision → GA in 6 weeks, Sep 2023

Team

Product, Eng, Content design, Data science, UXR

Tools

Figma

Overview

When ChatGPT arrived, it became an existential threat to Chegg's business — 50% of students were already using it. But Chegg had two advantages ChatGPT didn't: a network of human experts, and a decade of student behavior data. The goal was CheggMate, a conversational AI product built from scratch to retain that engagement.

The problem

This was uncharted territory on a fast timeline: one-week sprints, heavy ambiguity, requirements shifting as fast as the AI landscape did. I defined scope as we went, moving from vision to general release in roughly six weeks — replacing Chegg's core product by fall 2023.

Goals & constraints

Goal

Design and ship a 0→1 conversational AI product fast enough to matter

Goal

Make an AI chat interface feel guided, not intimidating, for first-time users

Constraint

1-week sprint timeline, evolving requirements, no existing pattern to build from

Process

I ran goal-setting, research, design, and validation on a tight, repeating loop across a series of one-week sprints. Four problems shaped most of the design work:

  • Dynamic Guides. Smart, contextual prompts, since students didn't know what they could ask an AI.
  • Universal Input. A dynamic keyboard that adapts to any subject — Chegg's first input component built to unify every input type.
  • Enriched content. UI consistency and multi-modal responses to bring flashcards, practice tests, and expert content into one thread.
  • A reusable system. Conversational UI guidelines and a design system other designers picked up immediately.

Outcome

CheggMate shipped as Chegg's new AI companion, built on GPT-4 and Chegg's network of 150,000+ subject-matter experts — covered by BusinessWire and MarketWatch, which noted the "very positive" feedback drove Chegg's shares up on announcement. In a diary study with 62 college students, CheggMate was described as helpful, accurate, and efficient. As Rahul, SVP of Content, put it: "The speed at which Kent was able to deliver the prototype was at new record speed at Chegg."
